Services &
Booking

Oteo (Alec Pontes) is an open-format DJ, House music producer and audio engineer based in Orlando. He is booked for residencies, club nights and private events, for production and engineering work, for branded content, and for equipment rental. Every booking is quoted per event.

Services

What Oteo is booked for.

DJ Sets & Residencies

Residency nights, club sets, weddings and private events. Open format for any room, with House at the core.

Gear Rentals

Pioneer DJ and AlphaTheta players and mixers, turntables and PA, as a full rig or by the piece, delivered and set up.

See the gear →

Music Production

Original tracks and remixes, produced, mixed and mastered to release standard.

Ghost Production

Finished productions delivered to a brief, unbranded, for release under another artist’s name.

Engineering

Mixing and mastering for artists who have recorded and need a finished record.

Branded Content

Short-form video and stills for brands, shot and edited by Oteo on location in Orlando and delivered ready to post or to run as an ad.

Lineup Booking

Booking the DJs, setting the running order and managing sound for an event.
