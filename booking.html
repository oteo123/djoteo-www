Questions

What does Oteo play?

Anything. That’s the perk of being an open-format DJ. He likes to play on a House beat and chooses what’s best for the party or the ambiance by reading the crowd. For corporate and family events he keeps it clean.

Does he bring gear?

Yes, or he plays on the venue’s CDJs.

Does he take requests?

Yes, when they fit the night.

How far ahead should I book?

As early as you can. Weekend dates go first.
