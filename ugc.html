Branded Content

Short-form, made in-house.

I write, shoot and edit vertical video for brands — on location, in natural light, delivered ready to post or run as an ad. No crew, no studio, one point of contact.

Orlando, FL English & Portuguese Cohley-verified creator Shot and edited in-house
Deliverables

What ships

Short-form video

15–60 seconds, vertical. Scripted, shot and cut. 4:5 and 1:1 versions on request.

Hooks and variants

Alternate openings on the same edit, so paid media has something to test.

Photography

Edited stills from the same shoot — product in hand, in use, in place.

Raw footage

Unedited selects delivered alongside the final cut.

Voiceover

English or Portuguese, recorded and mixed in post.

Usage rights

Organic included. Paid, whitelisting and exclusivity licensed by term.

Process

How a project runs

01

Brief

You send the product, the audience, and the one thing the video has to prove.

02

Concept

Two or three angles, with the opening seconds written out for approval.

03

Shoot

On location, natural light, product where it is used. Raw selects kept either way.

04

Delivery

Final cut in every ratio you need, plus raw footage. Two revision rounds included.

Background

Who you are hiring

Oteo is Alec Pontes — a Brazilian-American house producer and DJ based in Orlando, founder of Fish Out of Water Records, and a Cohley-verified content creator. Standing residencies at Debonair Supper Club and Casa Mezcal; past rooms include The H Orlando, Celine, Elixir, ICON Park and The Eye. Nominated for Best Club DJ by Orlando Weekly.
