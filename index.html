What kind of design work do you do?

I help startups and businesses design mobile apps, web apps, and digital products that are easy to use and built around real user needs.

What is your design process?

Every project starts with understanding your goals, users, and challenges. From there, I move into research, wireframes, UI design, prototyping, and testing before final delivery.

Do you work with developers?

Yes. I create organized design files, design systems, and documentation that make handoff smooth for developers and product teams.

Can you improve an existing product?

Absolutely. Through UX audits and usability reviews, I identify friction points, uncover opportunities, and provide clear recommendations to improve the user experience.

How long does a typical project take?

Timelines depend on the scope. Smaller projects can take a few weeks, while larger products with multiple screens and flows may take several months. I'll provide a clear timeline before we start.
