About

Alec Pontes, known as Oteo

DJ | Producer | Label Founder

Biography

Oteo’s musical journey began with his grandfather’s guitar in the Brazilian town of Timóteo. What started as childhood summers filled with acoustic rhythms eventually evolved into a definitive career in electronic music.

Since 2017, Oteo has been a driving force in the Orlando nightlife scene, establishing himself as a premier open-format DJ and House producer celebrated for his sophisticated, clean and high-energy curation. On the dance floor, his signature nights seamlessly bridge upscale environments with a driving House beat.

When he isn’t commanding the decks at elite venues, he runs Fish Out Of Water Records, the imprint where he releases his own heavy-hitting Afro House and Latin House productions.
