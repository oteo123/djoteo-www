Bio

Who You're Booking

OTEO is Alec Pontes — a Brazilian-American House DJ and producer based in Orlando, holding weekly residencies at The H and Debonair. He plays a warm, floor-first blend of House and Tech House, and reads a bilingual crowd equally in English and Spanish. As founder of Fish Out of Water Records, OTEO produces and spins his own original, fully-cleared music — the same sound live and on record.

Why Book OTEO

What You Get

  • 01Residency-tested. Weekly rooms at The H and Debonair — a set built and proven in front of real Orlando crowds, not a fill-in.
  • 02Bilingual crowd control. Reads and works the room in English and Spanish, so mixed rooms stay on the floor all night.
  • 03All-original, cleared music. Plays his own productions on Fish Out of Water Records — no licensing headaches, nothing to clear.
  • 04Can supply the rig. Full pro AlphaTheta / Pioneer setup available on request — one point of contact for talent and gear.
