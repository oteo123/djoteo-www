Bio

Who You're Booking

Oteo is Alec Pontes — a Brazilian-American House DJ and producer based in Orlando, holding standing residencies at Debonair Supper Club and Casa Mezcal. He plays a warm, floor-first blend of House and Tech House, and reads a bilingual crowd equally in English and Spanish. As founder of Fish Out of Water Records, Oteo produces and spins his own original, fully-cleared music — the same sound live and on record.

Why Book Oteo
