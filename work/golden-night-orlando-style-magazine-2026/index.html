The event

Golden Night celebrated the new issue of Orlando Style Magazine in the ballroom of The Citrus Club, high above Downtown Orlando. Florida Fashion Shows presented the runway collection of designer Gonzalo Calderón.

Oteo was the evening’s DJ, scoring the reception and the runway to the show’s creative direction, from arrival through the final walk. He provided the full sound and DJ production.
