Oteo · Experience

The rooms behind the work

Oteo (Alec Pontes) has played Orlando rooms since 2017, starting as one of the opening acts for Alok at House of Blues: standing residencies at Taverna Opa, The H Orlando, The Lawn Bar at Icon Park, Ava MediterrAegean, Helena Modern Riviera and Debonair Supperclub, and now Casa Mezcal. Orlando Weekly Best Club DJ nominee. Bilingual on the mic (Portuguese and English).
