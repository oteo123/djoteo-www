The night

Debonair Supper Club is a supper club with a dance floor in Downtown Orlando. From February 2024, Oteo played its Wednesday nights from the first seating to last call.

The first hour played to a seated dinner crowd, with the bass kept low. By midnight the tables were cleared and the dance floor was full.
