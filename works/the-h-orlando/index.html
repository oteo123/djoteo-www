The night

The H Orlando is a late-night dining room on Dr. Phillips Boulevard. Oteo was resident DJ for The Affair, its Saturday dinner party, from January 2021 to November 2022, and came back for a run of nights in May and June 2026.

He started at ten while dinner was still being served and picked the music up after eleven. By midnight the tables were cleared for a dance floor.

This is the room where he started building live mash-ups into his sets, often on a House beat.
