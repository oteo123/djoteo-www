Privacy

How this site handles what you send. Last updated November 28, 2026.

In short

What the forms collect

Your name, email, phone number if you add it, and what you write in the form.

Where it goes

The booking, rental and demo forms send your message through Web3Forms, a form delivery service, to oteo@djoteo.com. It is used only to reply and to arrange the booking. It is never sold or shared.

No tracking

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising trackers.

Embedded media

Recorded sets play from YouTube (in its privacy-enhanced mode, only after you press play) and SoundCloud, and the press kit is shown from Canva. Those services have their own privacy policies.

Your messages

Email oteo@djoteo.com to see or delete anything you have sent through this site.
