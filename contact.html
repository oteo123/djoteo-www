Questions

Booking, answered.

What kind of sets do you play?

Residencies, clubs, private and corporate events. I read the room and hold it till close. Plus custom mixes, gear rental, and original productions on Fish Out of Water Records.

How does booking work?

Send the date, venue, room and format. Oteo confirms availability and scope, then sends a quote. On the night, the set, mix and gear are dialed to the room.

Do you provide gear?

Yes — pro AlphaTheta / Pioneer DJ and PA gear, delivered and set up.

Can you play a room that's used to a different sound?

That’s the job — reading the room and building the night for the crowd in front of Oteo, rather than forcing a fixed setlist.

Can you take requests on the night?

Absolutely — reading the room includes reading the requests. If it fits the energy and the moment, I’ll work it in.

How far in advance should I book?

The calendar fills up around weekends and residency nights, so the earlier the better. Send your date and I’ll confirm availability right away.
