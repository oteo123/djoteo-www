Submissions

Send Us Your Track

FOOWR accepts demos for consideration. Send one private streamable link with clear artist and track details. A response is not guaranteed; if the track fits the label, you’ll hear back directly.

  • 01 One private, streamable link (SoundCloud / Dropbox) — finished or near-finished.
  • 02 Artist name, track title, and a line on the vibe.
  • 03 If the track fits FOOWR, you’ll hear back directly.
