Fish Out of Water Records

The label founded by Oteo — Alec Pontes. Warm, hands-up house out of Orlando. The same sound that runs the residencies, pressed into records.

The Label

Fish Out of Water Records

FOOWR is where Oteo releases his own productions and records by artists he plays with. Built in Orlando, aimed at the floor — the records carry the same warmth and low-end weight as the sets. Records made to move a room.

The Music

Edits, cut for the floor

Oteo’s edits rework familiar records for the room — the same open-house-format taste, sharpened for the dancefloor. 3310 and Pa’ Que La Pases Bien are out on Spotify; more lives on SoundCloud.

Submissions

Send Us Your Track

FOOWR accepts demos for consideration. Send one private streamable link with clear artist and track details. A response is not guaranteed; if the track fits the label, you’ll hear back directly.

  • 01 One private, streamable link (SoundCloud / Dropbox) — finished or near-finished.
  • 02 Artist name, track title, and a line on the vibe.
  • 03 If the track fits FOOWR, you’ll hear back directly.

The Sound

FOOWR is the label side of Oteo’s work: the same instinct that shapes the sets, put into original production. Follow the label on SoundCloud for new music.
